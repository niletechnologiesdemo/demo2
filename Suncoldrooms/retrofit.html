Retrofit questions

Retrofit FAQs

What is a solar + battery retrofit system?
A customised upgrade that allows existing coldrooms or refrigeration units to run on solar power. It combines photovoltaic panels with battery storage to provide reliable off-grid cooling — without needing diesel or mains electricity.
Can any coldroom be retrofitted?
Most standard coldrooms can be adapted with the right assessment and design, provided they're well-insulated with sound seals and structure.
How long does the retrofit process take?
Timeframes vary depending on the system and location, but most retrofits are completed within a few days to a week.
Will solar be enough to run my coldroom 24/7?
Yes. Our retrofit systems store energy efficiently in lithium battery banks, ensuring stable temperature control through night hours, cloudy conditions or low-sun periods.
What if I still need backup power?
We offer hybrid options that include optional backup generators or can be integrated with grid power if needed, giving you total flexibility for your operation.
What if my roof isn't suitable for solar panels?
We can explore ground-mount solar or split-array options depending on your site layout.
