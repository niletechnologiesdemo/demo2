Frequently Asked Questions

Simple answers to help you get started. Suncool offers a different way to cool things — here are the questions we hear most about how our systems operate, what's included, and whether they suit your situation.

General

What temperature do your coldrooms maintain?
All our systems are designed to hold a consistent 2°C temperature, provided loading and airflow are managed correctly.
Do I need access to power?
Not for day-to-day use. Our systems are solar powered with a battery bank designed for overnight operation.
Can your systems run at night?
Sure can! They are built to store enough battery power to run through the night without sunlight.
How long can they run without sun?
Typically overnight operation works fine. For extended cloudy weather, you can plug in mains or generator power via the integrated backup.
Are your coldrooms noisy?
They operate much quieter than a typical diesel setup, with no generator hum.

Renting a Coldroom

How do I book a rental?
Entirely online. Pick your dates on the live availability calendar, choose pickup or delivery, add any extras and pay securely by card. You'll get an instant confirmation and a PDF invoice — no phone calls or waiting for a call-back. Start a booking here.
What's the minimum hire period?
One day (a 24-hour window). You can hire for as long as you like — for bookings longer than a week we'll put together a custom quote with better rates.
How much does it cost?
Daily hire is $195. A weekend package (Friday to Monday) is $495 and a 7-day week is $1,150. Meat rails, shelving and load kits are optional extras, and delivery is calculated automatically from your address.
Do you deliver?
Yes. Pickup from our Gold Coast workshop is free, or we can deliver the trailer to your site and collect it afterwards. The fee is worked out per kilometre during booking, so there are no surprises. For sites beyond about 100 km we'll confirm delivery with a custom quote.
Is there a bond?
A $500 fully refundable security bond applies to every hire, collected at handover (not charged online). It's returned in full when the trailer comes back in good order — the same way car and equipment hire works.
Do I pay everything up front?
Yes, rental bookings are paid in full at checkout so your dates are locked in the moment you book.
Can I get a trailer with meat rails?
Yes — one of our trailers is fitted with meat rails for hanging carcasses after a kill. Select "meat rails" during booking and the system will check that trailer's availability for your dates.
What if I need to cancel?
You can request a cancellation from your account. Full refunds apply up to 48 hours before your hire starts; inside 48 hours we'll do our best to be fair, subject to the hire agreement.

Mobile Coldrooms

Can I tow the trailer myself?
Yes. We'll match the unit to your towing capacity and provide a full handover.
What sizes are available?
We offer a range of internal volumes to suit produce, drinks, supplies, or prepared food.
What's included in the mobile unit?
Each trailer comes with solar panels, battery bank, refrigeration system and LED lighting — plus optional shelving, meat rails and tie-downs.
Where do I collect from?
We're based in South East Queensland. Preferred handover is at our workshop, though delivery can be arranged.

Retrofits

Can you retrofit an old coldroom?
If it's well-insulated, with sound seals and structure, we can fit a solar + battery refrigeration package.
Do I need to remove my old equipment?
Not necessarily. In many cases we can leave existing systems in place.
What if my roof isn't suitable for solar panels?
We can explore ground-mount solar or split-array options depending on your site layout.
Will it still cool to 2°C after retrofitting?
Yes! We design the retrofit system to meet your required capacity.
