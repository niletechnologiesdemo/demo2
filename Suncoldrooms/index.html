How it works

Reliable off-grid cooling, powered by the sun

Four simple components work together to hold a steady 2°C — day and night, sunshine or cloud.

  • Solar panels capture energy from the sun to power the system.
  • Battery bank stores solar power to keep the coldroom running day and night.
  • 24V DC compressor delivers reliable, consistent cooling down to 2°C.
  • Digital controller lets you set and monitor the temperature at a glance.
  • Integrated backup accepts mains or generator power for extended cloudy weather.
