Questions we hear a lot

Mobile coldroom FAQs

How cold will it run?
Our mobile units are designed to hold as low as 2°C, with correct loading and airflow.
How long can it run without sun?
The battery bank is sized for overnight operation. For extended cloudy weather, plug in mains or generator power via the integrated backup charger.
Can I tow it myself?
Yes. We'll match the unit to your towing capacity and provide a full handover before you leave.
What maintenance is required?
Basic checks keep things tidy. We'll show you how and outline a service interval at handover.
Do I need a generator?
You won't need one most of the time, but as there will be times where solar may be affected, an outside source of power is recommended for peace of mind.
