Our story

An independent South East Queensland business solving off-grid cold storage

Suncool Coldrooms was founded by Gregg Chapman, a refrigeration specialist with more than 35 years across transport refrigeration, bus and mobile air-conditioning. After years of hearing the same thing from people on the land — "I need reliable cold storage, but I don't have power out there" — he built the answer.

Our systems hold a steady 2°C without any grid connection, with integrated backup power for cloudy periods or high-demand situations. Solar and battery do the work; a mains or generator input is there for peace of mind.
