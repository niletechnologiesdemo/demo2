About this drop

Learn the fundamentals of the third-shot drop, the most important transitional shot in pickleball. We break down grip, paddle face, and the body positioning that lets you keep the ball low and in the kitchen.

Recommended for players working their way from 2.5 → 4.0. Pair this with the Reset Footwork video for a complete transition-game module.
