Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 May 2026

1. Who we are

Goodhue Pickleball University ("GoodhuePB", "we", "us") provides a training platform for pickleball players and coaches. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. What we collect

  • Account data — name, email, phone, date of birth, address, emergency contact, profile photo.
  • Training data — sessions you log (Pickleball, Strength, Cardio, Recovery), skills, energy levels, notes, match outcomes.
  • External links — DUPR / Pickleheads / Pickleball Brackets profile URLs you choose to share.
  • Schedule data — appointments you create with coaches, and (if you connect) your Google Calendar tokens to sync events.
  • Usage data — pages visited, features used, device info — for product improvement.

3. How we use it

  • To operate the platform — show your sessions, summaries, schedule, and Matrix Utilization.
  • To enable coaching — let your coach see your progress and assign goals.
  • To send transactional emails — appointment confirmations, password resets, invitations.
  • To improve the product — anonymized analytics on which features players use.

4. Sharing

We share your training data with the coach(es) your account is associated with. We do not sell your data. Some technical services we use (email delivery, Google Calendar sync, cloud hosting) process data on our behalf under contracts that require equivalent protection.

5. Your rights

You can access, export, or delete your data at any time by emailing hello@goodhuepb.com. You can also disconnect Google Calendar from your profile.

6. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove personal data within 30 days, except where we're legally required to keep it (e.g. payment records).

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. We do not store passwords in plain text. Access by staff is limited to what's needed for support.

8. Contact

Questions? Contact us.
