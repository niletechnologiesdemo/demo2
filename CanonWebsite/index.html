Meeting & Event Production

When the lifts go up, ceiling tiles are coming out, conversations turn to beam placements, load ratings, and there is 4000 pounds hanging over the audience . . . EXPERIENCE COUNTS.

The types and extent of experience counts even more. We’ve worked in all areas of production from Stage, to Broadcast TV, to Corporate meetings. We bring a wide breadth of experiences and backgrounds to your next meeting. A meeting remembered, means a message remembered . . . and action follows. Create a memory for your audience and results will follow. Carry that experience beyond the venue, beyond the dates of your meeting, and gain even more return on your investment.

02

Staging

We offer complete staging services for meetings and tradeshows. We can design the set and stage and build it as well. Whether you have a theme to follow, or just want to make a statement, we provide you the best equipment, staging, personnel, and experience to do it.

Many of our clients also use us on simple meetings, screen and drape which we like to bring our own special brand to. No matter how big or how small your budget we are glad to help.

Production

Complete video production and post-production is available to you.
