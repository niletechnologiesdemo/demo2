MR
maya_rivers
5h ago · Preparation forum

I have my first master plant dieta in three weeks and I'd love to hear how others got their mind & heart ready. I'm feeling excited but also a lot of fear coming up.

How did you work with the nerves beforehand? Any journaling prompts, practices, or mindset shifts that helped you arrive open?

#preparation#firsttimer#dieta

bookmark Save

JK
josh_k ★ Elite
4h ago

The fear is part of the medicine arriving early. I'd gently suggest: lighten your diet a week ahead, reduce screens, and each night write one line — "what am I bringing to the plant?" By the time you arrive, you'll know your intention in your body, not just your head. You're not meant to feel ready. You're meant to feel willing. 🤍
