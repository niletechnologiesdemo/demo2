08

What changes from the current site

One deliberate reassignment
Current site

Pure black background with amber doing double duty — it’s both the button colour and the legal-warning colour, so neither reads clearly. The logo’s blue never appears.

Proposed

Lifted grey lets near-black product shots separate from the page. Shield blue takes every CTA, so amber is freed up to mean one thing only: read this, it’s the law.

09

Why this works for TCH

Your guns are black

On a black page the pistols dissolve into the background. A #14171C base gives the slide and grip an edge to sit against — the product reads first.

The blue is already yours

#0096FC isn’t invented — it’s lifted pixel-for-pixel from the shield. Using it for every action ties the whole interface back to the mark.

Red stays dangerous

Restricting #F60000 to the VAMPIR name keeps it loud. Spread across buttons it would go noisy fast, and it’s the wrong tone for a checkout.

Compliance has to be obvious

FFL and ATF copy is a legal necessity. Amber on a left rule makes it scannable and unmissable without shouting over the product.
