Built for Duty

In service since 2020

RS 9 Vampir is a semi-automatic pistol, caliber 9x19mm parabellum, with a magazine capacity of 18 rounds. This pistol was designed and manufactured in Republika Srpska, Bosnia & Herzegovina for professional use. Since 2020 it is used by the Special Anti-Terrorist Unit of Republika Srpska.

The RS9 Vampir is a full-size pistol, with grips made of a combination of polymer and industrial rubber, an attachment for the fifth finger (base plate) on the magazine, which provides the shooter with a secure grip when used in all weather conditions. Complete metal workmanship, outstanding functionality, superior precision and ease of use place the RS9 Vampir at the very top of choice when it comes to action in critical or tactical situations.
