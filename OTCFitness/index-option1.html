Option 1 · Library Led

The training content,
your data, your food
and the coach.

The athlete picks a guide from Steve's library and works through it in their own time. The app doesn't schedule them — it gives them the knowledge, reads their wearable, tracks what they eat, and answers questions about all of it.

12 interconnected screens No generated schedule Tap anything to navigate

Every discipline on the OTCF site, delivered as PDF, video and audio modules from the admin console.

Wearable data flows in automatically. Nothing to log, nothing to tick off.

Nutrition targets move with what the watch actually recorded, not with a prescribed session.

01

Getting started

Four onboarding steps instead of seven. Nothing needs collecting in order to build a plan, so the athlete reaches the library — and their first bit of value — far sooner.

02

The training library

In Option 1 this is the product. Guides are measured in modules and running time rather than weeks, and every module carries its PDF, video and audio.

03

Coach & nutrition

Unchanged from Option 2, and still the differentiator. The coach reads training, sleep and fuelling together — and can point the athlete at the module that answers their question.

04

Insight & conversion

Analytics reports what the athlete actually recorded. Without a plan there is no planned-versus-actual and no compliance figure — that is the one real analytical loss in Option 1.
