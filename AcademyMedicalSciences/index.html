A platform for enrolling, paying and learning — on the web and in the pocket

Three connected surfaces built around the academy's hybrid model: students discover a programme, pay online and study the theory wherever they are, then book the in-person practical at 10 George St N. Everything the academy publishes appears on the web app and the mobile app at the same time. The content, programme names, audiences, contact details and branding are the academy's own, taken from the flyer and website.
