Integrations

Six ways we make your site work for you

Your website shouldn't be an island. We connect it to the systems that run your business, so data flows automatically and nothing is keyed in twice.

01

Payments & e-commerce

Accept cards, PayPal, Apple Pay and more, with orders synced straight to your inventory in real time.

  • Card, wallet & PayPal checkout
  • Live order ↔ inventory sync
  • Subscriptions & secure payouts
02

CRM integration

Send leads, form submissions and customer activity directly into your CRM, and automate receipts and order confirmations.

  • Forms & leads pushed instantly
  • Full customer activity timeline
  • Automated receipts & confirmations
03

Marketing & automation

Turn visits into loyal customers with email and automation flows that fire the moment something happens on your site.

  • Automated welcome emails
  • Abandoned-cart reminders
  • Newsletter signups synced instantly
04

Scheduling & booking

If you take appointments, your website syncs with your calendar or booking system, no double-bookings, no back-and-forth.

  • Live calendar availability
  • Automated reminders & rescheduling
  • Syncs with your booking tools
05

Analytics & tracking

Understand exactly who visits your site and what they do, with clean, privacy-aware tracking and dashboards you'll actually use.

  • Visitor & conversion tracking
  • Goal & funnel reporting
  • Custom dashboards & events
06

Backend & ERP systems

For more advanced operations, connect your site to inventory management and ERP systems so the whole business stays in step.

  • Inventory management sync
  • ERP & accounting connections
  • Custom API & webhook pipelines
