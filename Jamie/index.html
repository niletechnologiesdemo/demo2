How the demo is built

What you are looking at

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No backend, no real payments, no database. Bookings, quotes and orders are seeded in the page and reset with the presenter bar. It exists to agree what gets built before anything is priced.

  • Presenter bar at the top of every demo jumps straight to any screen, public or admin.
  • The two trade sites share one design system and one back office shell, with a business switcher in the sidebar.
  • Photos are licence-free placeholders. Logos are Jamie’s own from Instagram. People and figures are fictional.
Suggested order on Monday

Twenty minutes, three stories

  • Militree first, five minutes. Home → survey request → client quote with deposit → Militree Ops pipeline and crew register. The recruiter line lands here.
  • Royal second, four minutes. Price list → book a slot with the over-65 toggle → Today view with the on-my-way button. Then the business switcher to show it is one back office.
  • Poseidon last, ten minutes. Home → retreat → free-place application → locked course → membership → shop → admin funding dashboard. Finish on the Sea to Summit Club page: Poseidon, Thor, Pegasus, and corporate team-building funding the therapy.
