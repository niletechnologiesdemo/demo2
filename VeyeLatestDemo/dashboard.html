Food Choices

Proteins

Proteins contain amino acids that are important building blocks for muscles and cells. Proteins are also important for satiety — letting the body know you have eaten and feel full. 30g of protein has the same effect in the body as new weight loss drugs like Ozempic. In addition, protein in sufficient quantities helps activate hormones that burn fat and decrease inflammation.

Fats

Healthy fats support hormones, brain function, and long-lasting energy. Prioritize monounsaturated Omega-9 fats like olive oil, avocado, almonds, and macadamia nuts. Avoid trans fats and limit pro-inflammatory Omega-6 vegetable oils. The right fats help signal satiety and support cell health.

Favorable Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates are the energy source for the brain, and a “no carb” diet can be harmful. All carbohydrates become blood sugar, and favorable carbohydrates are best for controlling blood sugar, therefore insulin, and therefore inflammation.

Unfavorable Carbohydrates

Unfavorable Carbohydrates enter the bloodstream quickly as sugar, and make the brain very happy. This is why it is easy to have carbohydrate addiction, your brain loves the sugar. The resulting spike in insulin causes an increase in inflammation — which can lead to disease. It is important to limit unfavorable carbohydrates.

Supplements

It is important to remember that a balanced diet is the first step to health management. Trying to undo or reverse the effects of an unbalanced diet with supplements will not work.

Although there are a myriad of supplements that can enhance your health, Veye recommends the two most important ones: Omega 3 fatty acids DHA/EPA, and Polyphenols.

The best source of DHA/EPA are pharmaceutical grade fish oils (not just purified or molecular distilled). For vegetarians and vegans, try an algae based DHA source. Omega 3 fatty acids reduce unresolved inflammation.

Polyphenols can be from berries, cacao, or other sources. High dose polyphenols allow for increased AMPK (metabolism) activity.

EPA/DHA suggested dosage

  • No chronic disease: 2.5g
  • Overweight/obese, type II diabetes, CHD etc: 5g
  • Chronic pain: 7.5g
  • Neurological disorders: 10g

Polyphenol suggested dosage

  • 500mg helps reduce oxidative stress
  • 1000mg helps reduce inflammation
