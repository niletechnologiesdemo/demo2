Terms & conditions

  1. Reservation, not ownership. The Buyer agrees to purchase the Product through scheduled instalments. The Seller reserves the Product but retains ownership and possession until all instalments are paid in full. The Product will only be dispatched after the final instalment is captured successfully.
  2. Zero interest, zero fees. No interest, account-keeping fees, or hidden charges apply for the duration of this agreement. The Total Payable amount above is fixed at signing.
  3. Automated payments. The Buyer authorises LayBys.com to automatically debit the Authorised Payment Method on the scheduled instalment dates. The Buyer will receive a reminder email three (3) days before each instalment.
  4. Failed payments. If a scheduled instalment fails, LayBys.com will retry the payment up to three (3) times over seven (7) days. The Buyer will be notified of each attempt.
  5. Cancellation by the Buyer. The Buyer may cancel this agreement at any time. On cancellation, all instalments paid to date (excluding the deposit) will be refunded within five (5) business days. The 20% deposit is forfeited as a restocking and reservation fee.
  6. Cooling-off period. The Buyer may cancel within 48 hours of signing without forfeiting the deposit.
  7. Cancellation by LayBys.com. If three (3) consecutive instalments fail, LayBys.com may cancel this agreement on the Buyer’s behalf. The Product will be returned to inventory and the Buyer will be refunded per clause 5.
  8. Stock and substitution. The Seller guarantees the Product is reserved for the Buyer for the duration of this agreement.
  9. Delivery. Once the final instalment is captured, the Seller will dispatch the Product within three (3) business days. Tracking will be provided via email.
  10. Returns and warranty. Standard Australian Consumer Law statutory rights apply. The Product carries the Seller’s standard 12-month warranty.
  11. Privacy. Personal data is handled per LayBys.com’s Privacy Policy and the Australian Privacy Principles.
  12. Governing law. This agreement is governed by the laws of New South Wales, Australia.
