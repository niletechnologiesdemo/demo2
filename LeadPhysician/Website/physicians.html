The curriculum

Eight modules. One transformation.

Short, structured video lessons with recorded live sessions and self-assessments — designed for a physician's calendar.

01Getting Started5 lessons · orientation & self-assessment
Your pre-evaluation, the program roadmap, and Dr. Koh's story — why a physician built this, and how to get the most from it.
02Improving Self-Image and Confidence9 lessons
Confidence as a daily practice: dismantling the inner critic that medical training installed, and rebuilding how you see yourself.
03Goal Setting and Achieving7 lessons · the A/B/C system
The A/B/C goal method with target dates, effort planning and accountability partners — the engine of the whole program.
04Overcoming Obstacles7 lessons
Reframing setbacks, navigating hospital politics, and moving forward when the system pushes back.
05Dealing with Fear and Focus9 lessons
Fear as information: presenting, negotiating and deciding under pressure without losing yourself in it.
06Living into the Future through Praxis8 lessons
Turning insight into repeated practice — the habits that make leadership automatic rather than aspirational.
07How to Make Friends and Influence People as Physicians10 lessons
Influence, relationships and communication for the realities of medicine — colleagues, administrators, patients, family.
08Where Do I Go from Here?3 lessons
Your leadership plan for the next year — and how the community, coaching and tools keep it alive.
