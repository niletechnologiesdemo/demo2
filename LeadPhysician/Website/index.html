Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Who is LEAD Community for?
Anyone who wants to lead better — at work, at home, or in their community. Students, parents, nurses, managers, volunteers. No title required. Physicians get their own flagship program with live coaching.
Is it the same content as the physician program?
The same proven curriculum, adapted for a general audience — the physician-specific examples become everyday ones. The method (self-image, goals, communication, resilience) is identical.
What exactly is LEAD Coach?
An AI coach for members, trained only on Dr. Koh's published lessons. Every answer cites the module it came from. It offers leadership guidance — never medical or mental-health advice.
How is the community kept safe?
Clear guidelines at the door, an active moderation team, and report tools on every post. Kind, real, constructive — or it doesn't stay up.
How do I start as a physician?
Schedule a free call. We'll help you pick the right tier — Silver for self-paced learning, Gold for group coaching, Platinum for private 1:1 work with Dr. Koh.
