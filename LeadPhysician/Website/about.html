FOUNDER & PHYSICIAN

Doctor. Leader. Teacher.

Elsie Koh, MD MHL, built LEAD on a simple conviction: the skills that make great leaders can be taught — and they should be available to everyone.

"You don't need permission to lead."— Dr. Elsie Koh
Her story

Leadership found her between cases.

For more than eighteen years, Dr. Koh has practiced Vascular and Interventional Radiology — a specialty where decisions are fast, stakes are high, and teamwork is everything. Along the way she took on leadership roles that medicine never trained her for.

So she trained herself: a Master of Healthcare Leadership at Brown University, coaching certification with the International Coaching Federation, and years of studying how adults actually change. Then she turned it into a program — first for physicians, and now for everyone.

MD · Physician MHL · Brown University ICF Member National speaker
The journey

From the reading room to the platform.

The training years Medicine, mastered

Medical school, residency, fellowship — and the discovery that clinical excellence alone doesn't make hospitals better places to work.

18+ years of practice Leading in the real world

Practicing Vascular & Interventional Radiology while taking on the leadership roles medicine never prepared her for — and learning what actually works.

The study years Brown MHL & coaching certification

Formal training in healthcare leadership and professional coaching — turning instinct into a teachable method grounded in educational psychology.

LEAD Physician® The program is born

An 8-module curriculum, group coaching and 1:1 work — the leadership program for physicians, by physicians. App, web platform and a growing cohort.

Next LEAD Community

The same method, opened to everyone: affordable courses, a safe community and a 24/7 AI coach. Because leadership was never just for titles.

What she believes

Three convictions behind everything.

Leadership is learned

Not a personality type, not charisma — a set of practices any committed person can build, one day at a time.

Whole-life leadership

The same skills that run a department well also raise a family well. Leadership shouldn't stop at the hospital door.

Access matters

Coaching is usually priced for executives. The community platform exists so price is never the reason someone doesn't grow.
