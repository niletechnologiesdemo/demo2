Leadership Guide
FREE The 5-Minute Leader
Dr. Elsie Koh, MD MHL · 6 min read

Most people think leadership begins with a promotion. In eighteen years of medicine, I learned the opposite: leadership begins in the small, unglamorous moments — how you enter a room, how you listen, and what you do in the first five minutes of your day.

"You don't need permission to lead. You need a practice."

— Dr. Elsie Koh

Try this tomorrow morning: before you check your phone, name one interaction where you want to show up differently. That single decision — made daily — compounds into presence.

Key takeaways
Lead before the titleInfluence starts with daily behavior, not position.
Five minutes, every dayOne intentional interaction beats one grand gesture.
Presence compoundsSmall consistent choices change how people experience you.

I have to give my manager difficult feedback tomorrow and I'm nervous about it.

LEAD Coach
That nervousness is normal — Dr. Koh calls it "the signal that the conversation matters." Her approach, in three steps:

1. Name the shared goal first — open with what you both want for the team.
2. Describe behavior, not character — one specific example beats a general complaint.
3. End with a request, not a verdict — invite them into the solution.
