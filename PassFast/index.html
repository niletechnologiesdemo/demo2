A cancellation policy that makes sense

Life happens. Your free-cancellation window depends on how soon your first lesson is — the further away, the longer you have to change your mind.

Lesson 5+ days away

Plenty of breathing room

24 hrs to cancel free

If your first lesson is five or more days away, you can cancel for free up to 24 hours after you book.

Lesson in 3–5 days

A solid window

8 hrs to cancel free

If your first lesson falls within the next few days, you have 8 hours after booking to cancel free of charge.

Lesson under 3 days

Last-minute bookings

2 hrs to cancel free

For lessons less than three days away, you can cancel free within 2 hours of booking.

After your free window closes, a cancellation fee applies. Full terms are shared at checkout.

Intensive courses mean lots of moving parts across multiple learners and locations. To make sure your instructor can reliably reach every lesson on time, we confirm your exact schedule manually within 12 hours rather than auto-booking slots that might clash.
