About us

Fifteen years of building software that organisations trust.

Nile Technologies was founded in 2011 by former members of the Oracle Fusion Middleware team. Today, 100+ engineers, consultants and project managers deliver enterprise, web, mobile and AI work for clients across the world.

HomeAbout Us

Who we are

Born in middleware. Grown into products and AI.

We started by building key strengths around Oracle Fusion Middleware and e-Business Suite for BPO, telecom, logistics, travel, manufacturing, energy, public sector and financial services clients. That integration DNA still runs through everything we do.

Over the years we added a dedicated open-source practice of 30+ certified developers for iOS, Android, React Native, React, Angular, Node.js, PHP, MongoDB and PostgreSQL, serving clients in the global market. In the last few years we have built a 40+ strong AI team working on generative and agentic systems for multinational clients.

What has not changed is the standard: certified people, professional project management, documented delivery, and the kind of after-care that earns a 91% repeat-order rate.

Our growth journey

From a middleware boutique to a three-practice technology partner.

2011

Founded in Noida

Former Oracle Fusion Middleware engineers set up Nile to deliver SOA, BPM and portal projects with a delivery standard the big consultancies could not match on price.

2013 to 2016

Enterprise integration at scale

Fusion Middleware programmes for GE Oil & Gas, Nucor Steel, HPSEBL and telecom operators; large data migrations and consumer portals.

2017 to 2019

Web, mobile and managed services

A dedicated open-source practice for global web and app clients, and long-term managed service contracts for enterprise portals.

2020 to 2023

Cloud integration and global products

2024 onwards

AI enablement
