About us

Seven generations
of savoir-faire

Lapostolle Wines was founded by Alexandra Marnier Lapostolle and her husband Cyril de Bournet in 1994. Alexandra is a member of the renowned Marnier Lapostolle family that has for generations dedicated itself to the production of high-quality wines and spirits.

In the creation of Lapostolle Wines, the family has maintained the same absolute commitment to quality that has always characterised them. The aim is as simple as it is ambitious: to create world-class wines by combining French winemaking philosophy with the magnificent terroirs of Chile.

Today Charles-Henri de Bournet, from the 7th generation of the family, is at the helm of the company.
